Post-trainer's Guide for Multilingual Synthetic Data Generation

A companion to Polyglot Teachers: Evaluating Language Models for Multilingual Synthetic Data Generation

By generating over 1.4M synthetic examples from 10 teacher models, and training 240 student models, we study the multilingual synthetic data pipeline and distill it into a recipe.
